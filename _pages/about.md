## Project overview

Cities are often represented too simply in global climate models. This project will use the **Local Climate Zones (LCZs)** framework to improve how urban form is represented, and to investigate what this means for urban climate projections and adaptation.

The work will develop global urban surface parameters based on LCZs for the Community Land Model – Urban (CLMU), integrate and evaluate an LCZ scheme in the Community Earth System Model (CESM), and apply the model to urban climate adaptation, including changes to urban surface albedo.

**Project:** [Global-scale modelling of urban climate projection and adaptation based on local climate zones](https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=UKRI1294)<br>
**Funder:** Natural Environment Research Council (NERC), part of UKRI<br>
**Host:** The University of Manchester<br>
**Funding period:** January 2026 – January 2029<br>
**Funding:** £798,000
